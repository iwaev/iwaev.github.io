Ever since humans first laid eyes on the night sky, they have been irremediably captured by the beauty of what nature had bestowed upon them. Countless cultures that have succeeded each other, various social and religious structures have always wanted to have a stake in the universe, because by tying ourselves to this sublime beauty, we feel special ourselves - and rightly so. These civilizations, even from millennia ago, have left breath-taking monuments that are a testament of their technological ability. And to this day, this continuous strive towards the exploration and the understanding of our universe brought us many technological marvels that are now essential in our society.
