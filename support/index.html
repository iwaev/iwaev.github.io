Support the IAYC

IWA e.V. is a non-profit organisation, and we try to make the IAYC as affordable as possible for participants. The camp is almost solely supported by participation fees and a small amount of revenue from merchandise sold at the camps themselves. Your generous donation will allow us to:

  • Keep the camp fee low
  • Purchase new equipment to keep the camp relevant for modern astronomy
  • Offer more camp fee grants to disadvantaged and minority participants
  • Maintain our web servers and cover administration expenses/overheads
  • Enthuse the next generation of astronomers!

Please note that IWA is a voluntary organisation. Leaders and organisational staff do not get paid. You can be assured that any donation or support you offer will directly benefit the participants of the camp.

Donations

We gratefully accept donations of any amount. Your contribution directly supports camp participants and helps keep the IAYC accessible to students worldwide.

  • PayPal - supports both one-time and recurring donations
  • Donorbox - optimized for recurring monthly/yearly donations
  • Bank transfer (Eurozone): Contact treasurer@iayc.org for details

Tax-deductible receipts available upon request.
